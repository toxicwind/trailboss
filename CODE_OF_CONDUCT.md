# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive: empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive feedback,
accountability. Unacceptable: sexualized language, trolling, insults,
harassment, doxxing, or other unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers via the
[trailboss issue tracker](https://github.com/toxicwind/trailboss/issues).
Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) v2.1.
